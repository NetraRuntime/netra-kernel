# gfx1151 BF16 QKV M=1 replacement (2026-07-31)

## Decision

Accepted for the fixed Qwen3.6 full-attention decode shape on **gfx1151**:

- `M=1, N=9216, K=2048`, BF16 row-major physical Q/K/V weight;
- raw kernel: `kernels/gfx1151/dense/qkv/bf16_qkv_decode_wave4_gfx1151.s`;
- symbol: `bf16_qkv_decode_wave4_gfx1151`;
- grid `(288,1,1)`, block `(256,1,1)`, wave32;
- HSACO SHA-256: `f5671248ce64bac53aba95e4c47d976aae0c3446bc98b4149e5687b59ab3006f`.

The SGLang guard is exact-shape and exact-dtype. `SGLANG_NETRA_ENABLE_BF16_QKV=0` restores rocBLAS. Prefill and M>1 remain on their prior paths.

## Why this was the next target

The complete eager M=1 trace ranked the ten full-attention QKV projections at a measured 6.025 ms per decode token on gfx1151, ahead of the remaining BF16 projection families. The rocBLAS kernel used 256 VGPR, 50 KiB LDS, and only about 10% measured occupancy at this shape.

The raw design assigns four output rows to each wave32. Every lane reads two adjacent BF16 K values, reuses the activation pair across four rows, accumulates with `v_dot2_f32_bf16`, reduces within the wave using `ds_bpermute_b32`, rounds to BF16, and stores four adjacent outputs. There is no LDS allocation, barrier, scratch, dynamic lookup, or allocation in the launch path.

## Real-weight correctness

All values below are **measured on gfx1151**, not estimated. Validation rotated through the ten real standard-attention Q/K/V weight sets (layers 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, and 39), a 377,487,360-byte working set.

- Maximum absolute difference versus PyTorch rocBLAS BF16: 0.015625.
- Maximum BF16 bit mismatches in one 9,216-element layer output: 17.
- Ten-layer graph replay maximum absolute difference: 0.015625.
- Candidate eager versus full-graph greedy sequences: 5/5 identical for exact 1+32 and 5/5 identical for exact 210+128.

The raw and rocBLAS algorithms are not bit-exact because their FP32 accumulation trees differ. Three real-layer FP64 spot checks found equal maximum error. Raw had slightly lower mean error in every spot check: it was closer to FP64 in 39 differing elements, rocBLAS in one. Some greedy sequences therefore differ from the rocBLAS baseline, but candidate eager and graph replay are deterministic and identical for matched inputs.

## End-to-end serving

Five samples per row, exact uncached requests, host wall time, dFlash disabled. All entries are **measured on gfx1151**.

| Graph | Exact tokens | Baseline median | Candidate median | Improvement | Candidate effective output tok/s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| disabled | 1+32 | 1721.365 ms | 1703.873 ms | 1.027% | 18.781 |
| disabled | 210+128 | 7170.624 ms | 7103.673 ms | 0.942% | 18.019 |
| full decode | 1+32 | 1732.380 ms | 1715.033 ms | 1.011% | 18.659 |
| full decode | 210+128 | 7227.319 ms | 7155.051 ms | 1.010% | 17.889 |

Cached tokens were zero in every sample. The non-streaming harness did not expose TTFT or separate input throughput; those fields are marked not measured in the JSON rather than inferred. Effective output throughput is derived from measured host E2E time and exact output count.

Full-graph capture was measured at 2.98 s baseline versus 2.86 s candidate. Both logs reported 0.16 GiB graph memory. The final candidate uses one stable 18 KiB output per full-attention layer, allocated during model construction; the observed available-memory drop across candidate capture was 0.08 GiB. Capture performs no module load, registry mutation, tensor query, or QKV output allocation.

Greedy sequences matched rocBLAS in 2/5 exact 1+32 eager cases, 4/5 exact 210+128 eager cases, 4/5 exact 1+32 full-graph cases, and 3/5 exact 210+128 full-graph cases. This is recorded as model-native BF16 numerical equivalence, not bit-exact equivalence.

## Negative variants

All negative results are **measured on gfx1151**.

- Wave8 Q reuse: bit-identical to wave4, but 7.5125 ms versus 5.2858 ms per ten-layer pass. Reducing activation traffic halved the wave count and lost memory-latency hiding. Rejected.
- Precise `vmcnt(3/2/1/0)` dependencies: bit-identical, but 5.2810 ms versus 5.2375 ms for the broad-wait wave4 kernel, a 0.8% regression. Rejected.
- Allocating graph wrapper: graph replay was correct, but startup available-memory deltas were noisy and could not prove capture-time allocation neutrality. Replaced with stable per-layer outputs before acceptance.
