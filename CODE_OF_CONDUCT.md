# Code of conduct

Netra Kernel contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful,
harassment-free community regardless of experience, identity, background, or
role.

Expected behavior includes:

- giving technically specific, constructive feedback;
- separating evidence from opinion;
- acknowledging uncertainty and unrun validation gates;
- respecting private data, credentials, and unpublished benchmark material;
- focusing disagreement on contracts, code, measurements, and reproducibility.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, fabricated results, or
sustained disruption of project collaboration.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
unnecessary disclosure about affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the NetraRuntime organization owners
through GitHub. Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose sensitive
personal information.
